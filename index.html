It was never supposed to be a series.

A friend of ours beat cancer. So we did the only reasonable thing: we filled a living room with disco balls and blue star balloons, put two DJs in the corner, poured something cold, and stayed up way too late.

We called it A.C.A.B: All Cancers Are Buajingan. Some things deserve a proper swear word.

The next morning people were already asking when the next one was. So the letters stayed and the meaning grew up:
